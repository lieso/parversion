The XPath engine supports a constrained grammar — keep generated xpaths

Use the simplest and most robust XPath 1.0 grammar possible to ensure a strict 1-to-1 match. specifically:
* Use semantic axes like `ancestor::tag[1]` instead of chaining multiple `../`.
